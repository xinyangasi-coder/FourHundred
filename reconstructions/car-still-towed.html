Reconstruction · A bill I cannot pay

The car was towed after a payment

Filed October 6, 2026 · Exam findings, not a named household · Not a person to copy

What this is. Findings from CFPB exams of auto servicers. No dialogue is invented.
Who should skip this. A paper that says the car is already in a court case. This page is not a defense.

Situation

This file serves I got a bill I cannot pay when the bill is a car repair or a car loan. In the 2025 SHED, a major vehicle repair or replacement was the most common large unexpected expense.

The shock

CFPB’s Fall 2024 Supervisory Highlights, published October 7, 2024, described exams of auto servicers. Examiners found cars repossessed after the borrower had made payments, or after a deferment, modification, or extension that should have stopped the tow. The order to tow was not canceled.

How the number changed

Two other findings in the same edition changed the price without a new repair. Subprime companies charged for optional add-on products the borrower had not agreed to buy. After a loan ended early, some servicers did not refund the unused part of the product. Some also applied a payment in a different order than the website disclosed, and a late fee followed.

The second cost was not another tire. It was a product, a fee, or a tow that the payment was supposed to prevent.

The fork

Before the tow, the open question is whether a payment or an extension was accepted, and whether an add-on was agreed. After the tow, the open question is the servicer’s record of that payment. This file does not name a company. The findings were directions to servicers after exams, not a judgment a reader can cite as their own case.

One step already on this site

If the repair is the bill that will not fit this month, use the one-bill path and the four-week grid. Write the payment date and the extension date before calling. This file does not add a negotiation script.

Do not borrow this

Examiners directed servicers to stop the practice and to refund late fees caused by the wrong payment order. That refund is an exam result. Waiting for a regulator to review a servicer is not a plan.

Source and date

CFPB, October 7, 2024 — Supervisory Highlights, Issue 35. Unnamed servicers.

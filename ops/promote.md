# Promotion prep

Not a growth calendar. Three audiences only: searchers in a bind, people who print a sheet for someone else, and reporters who need a definition on SHED day.

Monday Playbook stays on `ops/monday.md`. Do not use this file to invent extra pages.

## 0. Rule

Do not buy ads that say 37 percent of Americans cannot handle an emergency.
Do not add affiliate links.
Do not start a newsletter to prove the site is alive.
Do not pitch 211 or a library system for an official partnership this month.

## 1. You do this week — discovery

robots.txt already points at that file. Submission is a hint, not a guarantee.

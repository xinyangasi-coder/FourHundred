# Monday Playbook review

Private checklist. Not linked from the site. Do this before touching any other page.

Today’s date: ________
Decision: ship a new method / keep last week

## When you tell Grok on Monday

Send one of these two messages. Do not send a third kind.

**A. Ship**

> Monday Playbook. Ship: [working title]. Audience: [who]. Skip: [who should leave]. Source if any: [link].

**B. Keep**

> Monday Playbook. Keep last week.

If the title is missing on A, Grok proposes at most one method that fits Method rules and waits for a yes before writing. No list of five options. No “also fix footer while we are here.”

Grok then does only the three edits below. Method, Evidence, Foundations, and Start here stay closed unless you name a second task in a separate message.

## Rule

A new Playbook page ships only if it still meets Method: useful under pressure, dollar example, named skip, primary source or labeled example, no product pick.

If it does not, leave last week up. Do not write a page to fill Monday.

## Three edits if you ship

Do these three, in this order. If you only do one, stop and finish the other two.

1. **Playbook index** `playbook/index.html`
   - Move last week’s title from “This week” to “Earlier methods”.
   - Put the new title under “This week”.
   - Change “Last reviewed” to today’s date.

2. **Home** `index.html`
   - In “This week”, replace only the Playbook card (kicker stays Playbook).
   - Change the week line date.
   - In “How FourHundred is built”, keep the Monday sentence. Only change “This week: …” if the method title changed.

3. **Dates on the new page**
   - The new Playbook file has a reviewed date.
   - Do not change Foundation or Evidence dates unless a source actually moved.

## Three edits if you do not ship

1. Playbook index: leave “This week” as-is. Add one sentence under it if needed: “Reviewed Monday, [date]. No new method.”
2. Home: change only the week-of date in “This week”, not the Playbook card.
3. Touch nothing else.

## Do not do on Monday

- Do not rewrite Method.
- Do not shorten a long page to make the card fit.
- Do not update Evidence numbers because the week changed.
- Do not add a fourth Home card.

## After the three edits

Open fourhundredx.com/playbook/ and fourhundredx.com/ in a private window.
Confirm the same Playbook title is on both.
If they differ, the job is not done.

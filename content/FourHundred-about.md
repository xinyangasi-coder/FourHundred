## What this is

FourHundred is a U.S. money information tool for people already under financial pressure.

## How to flag an error

Email corrections@fourhundredx.com with the page address, the sentence or figure that looks wrong, and a source if you have one.

Do not send a Social Security number, an account number, a full statement, or a request for personalized advice.

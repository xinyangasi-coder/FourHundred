# FourHundred — Product Brief & Build Spec
Version: 0.1 · Audience: United States only · Status: name locked

---

## 1. One-page brief

**Name:** FourHundred  
**URL direction:** fourhundred.org preferred for trust; fourhundred.com if available  
**One-line position:** A U.S. money information tool for people already under financial pressure.

**What it is**  
A continuously updated display tool. It shows the information people with money problems most need to see, in three layers:

1. Foundations — core knowledge for managing personal finances  
2. Playbook — current, carefully screened methods  
3. Evidence — primary-source data about households under financial pressure  

It can also show high-quality AI-related personal finance knowledge and tools, with hard safety boundaries.

**What it is not**  
Not personalized financial, tax, or legal advice.  
Not a product marketplace.  
Not a debt-settlement or credit-repair company.  
Not an AI advisor that tells someone what to buy, borrow, or file.

**Who it is for**  
Adults in the United States who are already strained: behind on bills, carrying growing card balances, unable to cover a $400 shock with cash, living with little slack after rent, or earning a solid income and still tight.

**Who it is not for (primary)**  
People shopping for investment ideas, crypto, or “financial freedom” content.

**Job to be done**  
In under three minutes, help a visitor answer: *What should I look at first, given my situation?*

**Success metrics (launch)**  
- Situation-path completion (clicked a situation → reached a next action)  
- Return visits within 7 and 30 days  
- “This week” module click-through  
- Email / RSS subscribe  
- Source-click rate on Evidence cards  
Not vanity pageviews.

**Voice**  
Plain, specific, calm. Name costs in dollars. Show the definition behind every statistic. Never shame. Never promise a turnaround.

**Locked sentence for every page footer**  
FourHundred is an education tool. It is not personalized financial, tax, or legal advice.

**Brand use**  
- Wordmark: FourHundred (one word, capital F and H)  
- Do not style as “The Four Hundred” (that phrase historically meant a social elite)  
- Do not add “AI” to the brand name  
- Layers are product names, not the company name: Foundations, Playbook, Evidence

**Tagline options (pick one for launch)**  
1. Money information for people already under pressure.  
2. What to read when $400 would be a problem.  
3. Foundations. Better methods. Real numbers.

Recommended launch tagline: **Money information for people already under pressure.**

## 3. Home — first screen and page jobs

### First screen (mobile first)

**Eyebrow**  
United States · Education only

**H1**  
FourHundred

**Deck**  
Money information for people already under pressure.

**Support line**  
If a $400 bill would create a problem, start here. We show core money knowledge, current methods, and primary-source data. We do not give personalized advice.

**Primary chooser — “What is true right now?”**  
Buttons, equal weight, no ranking:

1. I may not make this month’s bills  
2. My credit card balance is growing  
3. I do not have a cash buffer  
4. I got a bill I cannot pay  
5. I earn enough and still run out  
6. I am late, or in collections  

**Secondary line**  
Or browse the three layers: Foundations · Playbook · Evidence

### Below the fold, in this order

**This week**  
Always three items, always one from each layer:

- Foundation to reread  
- One method, with who it is for / not for  
- One Evidence number that moved, or the number people misread most  

**Five gauges** (Evidence, human language, source on the card)

1. $400 shock — share who would not use cash or its equivalent  
2. Bills — share who did not pay every bill last month / year  
3. Unmanageable debt  
4. Card balances rising fastest among people already struggling  
5. Most common surprise expense (car, home/appliance, medical)

**How FourHundred is built**  
Three short columns: Foundations stay stable. Playbook updates weekly. Evidence updates when the source updates.

**AI, in one box**  
AI can help you understand a term or draft questions. It should not decide a product, a filing, or a transfer. See the screened list.

**Footer legal**  
Education only. Not personalized financial, tax, or legal advice. Figures link to primary sources. Definitions matter; the same headline can describe different groups.

## 4. Page templates

Every content page uses the same skeleton so weekly publishing stays possible.

### Shared header
- Layer label: Foundation / Playbook / Evidence  
- Title  
- Last reviewed date  
- Time to read  
- “Who this is for” in one sentence  
- “Who should skip this” in one sentence  

### Shared footer
- Next action (one thing, 10–20 minutes)  
- Related Foundation / Playbook / Evidence links  
- Sources  
- Education disclaimer  

---

### Layer 1 — Foundation template

**Page job:** Give a shared language and one small action. Do not teach a whole course.

**Required sections**

1. In one sentence  
2. Why this matters if money is already tight  
3. The real-cost example (dollars, not slogans)  
4. The smallest useful version  
5. Common mistakes  
6. What not to do  
7. When this is not enough (point to a human or official help)  
8. Go next: one Playbook method + one Evidence card  

**Launch six Foundations, in this order**

1. Cash flow: money in, money out, money left  
2. The $400 test and the three cash buffers ($400 / 1 month / 3 months)  
3. How a minimum credit-card payment works  
4. Which bills to protect first  
5. A 7-day cash map  
6. Scams, debt-relief ads, and “AI will fix this” flags  

---

### Layer 2 — Playbook template (weekly)

**Page job:** One method. Named limits. No guru tone.

**Required sections**

1. The method in one paragraph  
2. For you if…  
3. Not for you if…  
4. Steps  
5. A worked dollar example  
6. What can go wrong  
7. Official or primary-source backing, if any  
8. Related Foundation  
9. Related Evidence  

**Editorial rule**  
If a method needs someone’s full tax return, risk tolerance, or a product recommendation, it does not ship.  
Investing methods stay out of the weekly slot until Foundations 1–6 exist.

**First Playbook entry to write**  
“Why the minimum payment keeps a card balance alive.”

---

### Layer 3 — Evidence card template

**Page job:** Translate a statistic for the person inside it. Never use a scary headline without a definition.

**Required fields**

- Plain title  
- The number  
- Date and source (agency + publication name + link)  
- Exact definition / survey question, in plain English  
- Who is in the number, who is not  
- What changed since the last release  
- What this does *not* prove  
- What to read next (Foundation or Playbook)  

**Launch five cards**

1. Fed SHED: paying a $400 expense with cash or its equivalent  
2. Fed SHED: missed bills / food hardship / skipped medical care  
3. Financial Health Pulse: financially vulnerable households; unmanageable debt  
4. NY Fed / SHED credit match: card balances rising among people “finding it difficult to get by”  
5. Fed SHED: most common unexpected expenses (vehicle, home/appliance, medical)  

**Update rule**  
Evidence pages change when the source changes, not because the news cycle wants a new take.

## 5. AI tools page — boundaries

**Allowed**  
- Explain a Foundation term  
- Compare two *methods already published on FourHundred*  
- Turn a pasted (non-account) spending list into categories  
- Draft questions to ask a card issuer, hospital billing office, or nonprofit counselor  
- Help a reader use FourHundred’s own pages  

**Not allowed**  
- “Which card should I apply for?”  
- “Should I file bankruptcy?”  
- “What stock / fund / coin should I buy?”  
- Connecting live bank, brokerage, or credit accounts to a chatbot  
- Uploading SSNs, account numbers, or full statements into public models  

**Tool-wall rule**  
A tool is listed only if access is read-only, the privacy policy is readable, and the listing says what the tool cannot do. Maximum three listings at launch.

## 6. Publishing cadence

| Cadence | What ships |
|---|---|
| Weekly, same weekday | One Playbook + refresh Home “This week” |
| When a source releases | Only the affected Evidence card |
| Monthly | Review one Foundation; review AI tool wall |
| Optional daily | 80-word “today’s number or myth,” linking to an existing page |

Do not promise daily longform.

## 8. Design notes for FourHundred

- Mobile first. Situation buttons are the hero, not a market ticker.  
- Wordmark and one color for state only: stable / strained / worse.  
- Every number sits next to its source.  
- Body text 16px+, high contrast, reduced-motion option.  
- Fast over decorative. No stock photos of smiling couples with laptops.  
- Date stamp visible on every page.

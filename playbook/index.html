Methods

  • Make one hardship call and write down the answer — one company, one amount you can send, four lines.
  • Stop new charges on one card for one cycle — so the next statement shows interest without new purchases.
  • Read the validation notice before you answer a collector — 30-day written dispute; official letters only.
  • Put this month’s due dates on a four-week grid — paycheck and due date, not a ranking.
  • Search official unclaimed property, then your own old logins — free search; median claim is small.
  • Mark the drafts that hit before payday — pause only what is not housing, work, or medicine.
  • Ask to move the due date so the paycheck and the minimum share a week — a courtesy request, not a federal right.
  • Why the minimum payment keeps a card balance alive — current is not the same as paid off.
  • Filter a money question before you paste it into a chatbot — rounded categories; check dates on official pages.

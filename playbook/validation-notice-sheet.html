Validation notice sheet

· Method

One notice only. If the paper says summons or complaint, stop. This sheet is not a court response.
LineFrom the notice
Collector name
Creditor named
Current amount
End date of the 30-day period

Circle one: I do not recognize this / the amount looks wrong / I only want calls to stop / I recognize it and the amount matches

Letter I will use: tear-off on the notice / CFPB sample / none today

A written dispute before the date on the notice can pause collection of the disputed part until they send verification. Asking them to stop calling does not erase the debt. Do not send a chatbot letter, a Social Security number, or a bank login. Samples: CFPB debt collection.

I am late, or in collections

Last reviewed September 29, 2026 · 30–40 minutes

Who this is for. Adults in the United States who missed a payment, received a collection call or letter, or saw an account leave the original company.
Who should skip this. People whose accounts are current, or who already have a lawyer handling a case.

Name the stage

  • Late with the original company
  • A collector is contacting you
  • Court or lockout paper is here

If you cannot cover rent, utilities, food, or medicine this month, use I may not make this month’s bills in the same sitting.

If you are late with the original company

Pay nothing extra toward this account until housing, essential utilities, food, and required medicine are covered. If cash remains, send the minimum on time if you can, call the original company, and write down what they said.

If a collector is contacting you

Collectors generally have to give you validation information when they first communicate, or within five days of first contact. Read the paper before you talk. If you do not recognize the debt, CFPB says you can dispute in writing within 30 days of receiving the validation information.

Do not send money to a company that promises to wipe the debt for a fee before you have read CFPB pages on debt relief.

If court paper is here

Stop using this page as the main tool. Read the deadline. Contact legal aid. If the paper is about housing, also call 211. Do not take a payday loan to make the lawsuit go away on a phone promise.

Pick the sentence that is true today. These paths are for this month, not for a lifetime plan.

Who this is for. Adults in the United States already under pressure.
Who should skip this. Readers who only want a definition. Use Evidence or Foundations.

Live paths

  • I may not make this month’s bills — several essentials this month may not clear. Printable sheet.
  • I got a bill I cannot pay — one invoice that will not fit, while the rest of the month is still mostly standing.
  • I do not have a cash buffer — a few-hundred-dollar surprise would go on a card.
  • My credit card balance is growing — the number is rising or not falling while you pay.
  • I earn enough and still run out — the paycheck looks fine and the month still ends empty.
  • I am late, or in collections — name the stage: original company, collector, or court paper.
  • I have a hospital bill I cannot pay — itemized bill first, then the hospital’s financial-assistance policy.

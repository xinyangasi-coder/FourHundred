I may not make this month’s bills

Last reviewed September 29, 2026 · 30–45 minutes today

Who this is for. Adults in the United States who already know this month’s money may not cover every bill.
Who should skip this. People whose bills are current and who only need a buffer or interest explanation.

What this page is for

This is a path for this month, not a plan for the rest of your financial life. The job is: keep shelter, power, food, medicine, and the ability to earn a paycheck, then decide what can wait.

If eviction, foreclosure, utility shutoff, repossession, or a lawsuit is already in motion, use the official links below and get a person the same day.

Do this first (10 minutes)

  1. Cash and money that will actually arrive before the next due date
  2. Bills already due or due in the next 14 days
  3. Which of those bills, if unpaid, would take housing, utilities, medicine, or the way you get to work
  4. Any letter that mentions eviction, foreclosure, shutoff, repossession, wage garnishment, or a court date

If line 4 is not empty, that item jumps the list.

A working order when cash cannot cover everything

Protect first: housing; essential utilities; food and required medicine; transport and insurance required to keep a job; court-ordered payments and tax debts that already have enforcement.

Keep current if anything is left: minimum payments on accounts still open and not in collections.

Usually later: extra payments on cards or personal loans; subscriptions that can be paused.

A collector can sound urgent. An eviction notice is a different category.

What to do with each unpaid bill

Call or write before the due date if you still can. Name an amount you can actually send. Write down the date, the name, and what they said. Do not promise an amount that would bounce rent.

Housing and utility help are local. CFPB points renters to local organizations and to 211. Energy help often starts at LIHEAP or 866-674-6327.

What not to do this month

  • Do not take a payday or title loan to make a card look current while rent stays unpaid.
  • Do not empty a retirement account to clear unsecured bills without a tax professional.
  • Do not pay the loudest collector first just because they called twice.
  • Do not connect live bank accounts to a public AI chatbot.
  • Do not ignore a court summons.

I earn enough and still run out

Last reviewed September 29, 2026 · 30–40 minutes

Who this is for. Adults in the United States whose pay looks fine on paper and whose month still ends empty, short, or on a card.
Who should skip this. People who cannot cover this month’s essentials, or who only need card-balance math.

What this page is for

A solid paycheck and a tight month can exist together. The month usually fails in timing, fixed costs, small shocks, or quiet leaks. This page is a sorting path, not a lecture about lattes and not an investing plan.

Do this first

Use take-home pay. Write one month: money that arrived; housing; hard-to-pause bills; leftover last month; card balance change last month. Circle whether leftover is near zero before any surprise, dies by week three, or disappears into a card balance.

A working order

  1. If housing or food is at risk, use the bills path.
  2. Fix timing before you cut identity. Ask whether a due date can move.
  3. Name the fixed-cost share: housing plus hard-to-pause bills divided by take-home pay.
  4. If leftover is dying on a card, use the growing-balance path.
  5. Build Rung 1 of the $400 buffer only from true leftover.
  6. Audit subscriptions after the structure is visible.
